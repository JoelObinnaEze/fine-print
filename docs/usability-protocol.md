# Five-minute usability protocol

Use three to five volunteers who have not seen Fine Print. Do not explain the product before the tasks. Record only task times and answers; do not collect names or unnecessary personal data.

## Prompt

“This tool tests reward rules before launch. Please use the public demo and think aloud. I cannot help you until the end.”

## Tasks

1. In one sentence, what problem does Fine Print solve?
2. How much reward was paid in the commerce example, and how much value did the business keep?
3. What repair does the tool recommend, and what does an honest customer lose?
4. In Bottle Return, did the points correspond to actual recycling?
5. What decides whether a proposed sequence really worked: Muse or the simulator?

## Success thresholds

| Measure | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Correct product explanation | 4/5 participants |
| Find commerce result | 5/5 within 30 seconds |
| Find recommended repair | 4/5 within 60 seconds |
| Identify simulator as verifier | 4/5 |
| Complete without assistance | 4/5 |
